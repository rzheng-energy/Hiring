The university

Founded 1896 · C9 League · Xi'an, Shaanxi

A research university with a long engineering tradition, and an unusually strong concentration of energy and materials expertise.

Xi'an Jiaotong University is one of China's earliest modern universities and a member of the C9 League. Its materials programme was among the first national key disciplines, and the university holds Double First-Class status in the field.

The campus for this appointment is iHarbour — the Western China Science and Technology Innovation Harbour — where the materials research institute opened in 2019 with 31,500 m² of dedicated space, alongside the national platform for energy-storage industry–education integration established in 2021.

What we are hiring for

Five directions, one group

You do not need to cover all five. We are looking for depth in one, and curiosity about the rest.

  • Energy storage materials and devices

    High-energy electrodes, solid electrolytes, functional electrolytes, flow batteries, thermal management and safety, recycling and second life.

  • Hydrogen across the chain

    Water electrolysis, fuel cells, hydrogen storage — solid-state, liquid organic carriers and clathrate hydrates — plus safety and system integration.

  • Photovoltaics and solar fuels

    High-efficiency photovoltaic materials and devices, photocatalysis, electrocatalysis and solar-driven chemical conversion.

  • Structural and functional materials

    Lightweight metals and alloys, inorganic non-metallics, glass and high-performance fibres, composites, two-dimensional materials.

  • Materials design and characterization

    Multiscale modelling, machine-learning-guided discovery, in-situ and operando characterization, and the interfaces between them.
